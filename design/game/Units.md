# Units
Units are the controllable entities of each player.  
There are different kinds of units, each with their own roles, abilities, and strategic purposes.  
Managing the right mix of units is essential to building a strong economy, defending your territory, and launching successful attacks.

## Servant
Servants transport materials between production buildings, such as carrying grain from farms to mills or delivering logs to lumbermills.  
They are the logistical backbone of your settlement, ensuring that all resources reach the right place at the right time.

## Builder
Builders are responsible for constructing new buildings and repairing damaged structures.  
They form the core of your base development and must be protected to ensure continued expansion.

## WoodCutter
WoodCutters specialize in harvesting wood from forests.  
They cut down trees providing one of the most important construction resources.

## Carpenter
Carpenters process logs into planks at the lumbermill.  
Their craftsmanship supplies the essential building material needed for advanced construction and tools.

## Stone Mason
Stone Masons mine and cut stone from rocky deposits.  
Their refined stone is required for solid and durable structures.

## Farmer
Farmers cultivate fields to grow grain or may raise animals such as pigs or horses.  
They provide the foundational food sources that support both the population and livestock.

## Miller
Millers process harvested grain into flour at the mill.  
This refined ingredient is essential for bakeries to produce bread.

## Baker
Bakers use flour to produce bread, a critical food source for your workers and troops.  
Their output helps maintain morale, efficiency, and overall unit wellbeing.

## Butcher
Butchers slaughter pigs to produce meat and skins.  
Their work contributes both high-quality food for taverns and essential materials for further processing.

## Tanner
Tanners process skins into leather.  
Leather is a crucial resource for crafting reinforced armor and other durable equipment.

## Miner
Miners extract valuable raw materials from underground deposits, including coal, iron ore and gold ore.  
They are essential for advancing into the metalworking stage of your economy, supplying the resources needed for smelting, forging, and crafting advanced equipment.

## Blacksmith
The Blacksmith processes raw metals into refined bars by smelting iron ore and gold ore using coal.  
Refined metal bars form the backbone of advanced crafting, enabling the production of weapons, armor, and other high-tier military equipment.

## Axe Fighter
Axe Fighters are frontline melee units equipped with an axe and a shield.  
They excel in close-range combat and serve as durable ground troops capable of holding the line.

## Spearman
Spearmen wield long spears, making them effective against cavalry and fast-moving enemies.  
They provide reach, formation capability, and reliable defense against charges.

## Bowman
Bowmen are ranged units who attack from a distance using bows.  
They excel at harassing enemies, supporting frontline troops, and picking off key targets before the battle closes in.

## Scout
Scouts are fast, lightly armed units—typically mounted with an axe.  
They are ideal for exploration, hit-and-run tactics, and gathering information about enemy positions and movements.

### Craftsman
The Craftsman is a versatile worker responsible for producing military equipment and essential gear.  
Skilled in working with wood, leather, and simple metal fittings, the Craftsman creates the weapons and armor needed to outfit your troops.

Craftsmen craft items such as axes, bows, spears, and wooden shields, as well as assemble leather armor.  
Their workshop serves as the backbone of your army’s equipment production, ensuring soldiers are properly armed and protected.


## Caravan
The Caravan is a special starting unit granted to every new player.  
It serves as the foundation of your first settlement and represents your initial foothold in the world of Cwars.

The Caravan can move across the world to help players find a safe or strategically valuable location.  
Once a suitable spot is chosen, the player can deploy the Caravan.  
Deploying it permanently converts the caravan into the player's first settlement core, creating:

- 1 Storage
- 3 Serves
- 3 Builders
- 15 Gold

After deployment, the Caravan disappears, and the newly spawned workers and Storage form the basis of your emerging economy.  
Protecting these first units is crucial, as they determine your early survival.
