# Resources
Resources are the fundamental materials required to grow your settlement, train units, and expand your influence.  
Each resource serves a unique purpose, and effective resource management is crucial for maintaining a strong economy and sustaining your strategic plans.

## Log
Logs are obtained by cutting down trees.  
They serve as the raw material for carpenters, who process them into planks at the lumbermill.

## Planks
Planks are the basic construction material used for building structures, crafting tools, and creating early machinery.  
They are produced by converting logs at the lumbermill.

## Stones
Stones are extracted from rocky deposits.  
They are essential for constructing durable buildings, fortifications, and other sturdy structures.

## Grain
Grain is grown in fields and harvested by farmers.  
It serves as the foundation of your food economy and is used to feed both people and livestock.

## Flour
Flour is produced by milling harvested grain.  
It is a refined ingredient used in bakeries to produce bread, a primary food source.

## Bread
Bread is baked using flour and serves as a staple food for your population.  
Proper food production keeps workers efficient and units healthy.

## Pigs
Pigs are livestock animals fed with grain.  
They are raised over time and eventually butchered to provide valuable meat.

## Meat
Meat is a high-quality food resource consumed in taverns or supplied directly to soldiers.  
It boosts morale and helps maintain the strength of your troops.

## Skins
Skins are obtained as a byproduct when pigs are butchered.  
They serve as the raw material for producing leather.

## Leather
Leather is crafted by tanning skins.  
It is used to create reinforced armor, protective gear, and other advanced equipment.

## Horses
Horses are bred and raised to serve as the foundation for fast-moving military units.  
They enable cavalry troops to travel quickly across the battlefield, perform scouting missions, and execute rapid flanking maneuvers.  
Maintaining a healthy stable of horses is essential for any strategy that relies on speed, mobility, or surprise attacks.

## Leather Armor
Leather Armor is crafted from tanned leather and provides basic protection for military units.  
It offers increased survivability without significantly hindering movement, making it ideal for early- to mid-game troops.

## Axe
Axes are melee weapons forged for frontline combat units.  
They provide strong close-range damage and are commonly used by infantry specializing in offense and breaching enemy defenses.

## Bow
Bows are ranged weapons crafted from wood and string.  
They allow units to attack from a distance, making them essential for skirmishing, supporting frontline troops, and weakening enemies before close combat begins.

## Spear
Spears are long, pointed weapons used by infantry for reach and formation fighting.  
They are particularly effective against cavalry units and provide strong defensive capabilities in tight formations.

## Wooden Shield
Wooden Shields are basic defensive equipment crafted from planks.  
They help melee units block incoming attacks, increasing their durability and allowing them to hold the line more effectively during combat.
