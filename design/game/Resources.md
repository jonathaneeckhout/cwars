# Resources
Resources are the fundamental materials required to grow your settlement, train units, and expand your influence.  
Each resource serves a unique purpose, and effective resource management is crucial for maintaining a strong economy and sustaining your strategic plans.

## Wood
Wood is a fundamental resource used primarily for constructing buildings and crafting basic weapons. It serves as the backbone of early expansion and military development.  
Players can obtain wood by cutting down trees scattered throughout the world, either manually or through automated worker units.

## Food
Food is essential for sustaining all living units in the game. Workers, creatures, and soldiers require a steady food supply to survive.  
If food runs out, your population will begin to starve, reducing their effectiveness and eventually leading to death—making food management a crucial part of strategic play.

## Gold
Gold is the most valuable and sought-after resource. It functions as the main currency of the world and is required for training advanced units, constructing high-tier buildings, and engaging in trade with other players.  
Securing a reliable gold income is key to maintaining an economic and military advantage.
