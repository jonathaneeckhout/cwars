# Stats
Stats define the core attributes of each unit.  
They determine a unit’s effectiveness in combat, survivability, and overall performance within your settlement or army.

## Health
Represents the unit’s life.  
Units take damage in combat or from starvation.  
When Health reaches 0, the unit dies.

## Attack Power
The unit’s standard attack strength.  
Determines how much damage the unit deals during normal attacks.

## Pierce Attack
Bonus damage specifically effective against mounted units.
This stat makes certain units, such as spearmen, more effective against cavalry.

## Defense
Reduces incoming damage from enemy attacks.  
Higher Defense allows units to survive longer in battle and resist melee or ranged hits.

## Movement Speed
How fast a unit moves across the world.  
Faster units traverse terrain quickly, scout more effectively, and respond efficiently to threats.

## Range
The distance from which a unit can strike or shoot.  
Applies mainly to ranged units (e.g., bowmen), determining how far away they can attack enemies.

## Food
Represents the unit’s current level of nourishment.  
- **100%** → fully fed  
- **0%** → starving and gradually dying  
Units consume food over time and must eat regularly in Taverns or other food-providing buildings.

## Age
The unit’s current age.  
As units age, they move closer to their natural lifespan.

## Max Age
The maximum age the unit can reach.  
This value is generated when the unit is created.  
Once the unit reaches its Max Age, it dies of old age.
