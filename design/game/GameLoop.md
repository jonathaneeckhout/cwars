# Game Loop
The game loop of Cwars describes the natural flow of a player’s journey—from spawning into the world, to building and expanding a thriving settlement, to ultimately surviving or falling.  
Players progress through exploration, economic growth, diplomacy, and warfare, all while managing their units and resources in a persistent shared world.

## Start
When a new player creates an account, they are invited to enter the world of Cwars.  
Each player spawns at a random location on the map, accompanied by a Caravan, their first and most important unit.

The Caravan acts as a mobile starting base, allowing the player to explore the surrounding terrain and choose where to establish their settlement.  
Once the player finds a suitable location, they can deploy the Caravan, immediately creating a Storage, three Serves, three Builders, and granting 15 Gold.  
From this moment on, the player’s settlement becomes permanent, and the true journey begins.

## Main
The main phase of the game focuses on survival, expansion, and interaction with the world.  
Players must grow their economy, construct buildings, produce goods, train workers and military units, and gradually transform their settlement into a flourishing domain.

The world is shared with many other players, leading to opportunities and dangers.  
Players can forge alliances, create trade partnerships, defend their territory, or wage war.  
Exploration, diplomacy, resource management, and strategic planning all play crucial roles in achieving long-term success.

Whether advancing through cooperation, economic power, or military dominance, players shape their own path in Cwars.

## End
A player's journey ends when all their units are destroyed.  
Without workers, military forces, or a Caravan to rebuild from, the player loses all presence in the world.  
At this point, the player can start a new journey with a fresh Caravan and try again to carve out their legacy in Cwars.
